# Minimum moderation and appeals operations

Technical workflow: reports are classified as imminent threat/child safety, severe abuse, privacy/identity, harassment/spam, or ordinary policy; least-privilege roles separate triage, report review, identity evidence and safety actions; every sensitive read/action requires purpose and audit evidence. Blocking is immediate user control and does not substitute for review.

Proposed targets (not commitments until staffed): imminent threat/child-safety escalation immediately during coverage; severe abuse within 4 staffed hours; ordinary reports within 2 staffed days; appeals acknowledged within 2 staffed days and decided by a reviewer independent of the original action. Preserve evidence only as policy allows. The service is not emergency response; direct immediate danger to local emergency services.

Required named roles: primary triage moderator, backup/out-of-hours escalation owner, independent appeal reviewer, child-safety/legal escalation contact, privacy/deletion owner and incident commander. Current 19:00–22:00 Asia/Bangkok owner coverage is insufficient for a public SLA. Until assignments and training records exist, this blocker remains owner-required and public Social stays closed.
