## 1. Eligibility and accounts

The proposed service is limited to people aged 18 or older. Age is self-declared and may be reviewed or restricted for safety; ChatGun does not claim independent age verification. Users must control the OAuth account they use, provide truthful profile information, protect sessions and devices, and promptly report unauthorized access. Google, Facebook, and LINE are independent providers with their own terms.

## 2. Social service

Discovery and approximate-location processing are voluntary. Friendship is mutual: a request must be accepted before text contact. Consent to friendship or messaging can be withdrawn by removing the friend, disabling contact, blocking, deleting the account, or leaving the service. Online Presence is optional and may be stale. Ratings, Media, Sensitive Content, OTP, and other gated features are available only when separately approved and enabled.

## 3. User content and conduct

Users retain their rights in profile text, messages, reports, and permitted uploads, while granting ChatGun only the limited rights necessary to host, transmit, secure, moderate, back up, and delete that content. Final license wording and termination effects require legal approval. Illegal material, harassment, threats, stalking/location inference, impersonation, child exploitation, NCII, block evasion, spam, fraud, malware, unauthorized access, and abusive reporting are prohibited.

## 4. Enforcement and appeals

ChatGun may restrict a feature, quarantine content, suspend an account, preserve narrowly necessary evidence, or terminate access to protect users, enforce these rules, or comply with law. Decisions should be proportionate, audited, communicated where safe, and appealable through the approved support channel. A reviewer should not decide their own case; high-risk or legal matters require the designated safety/legal owner.

## 5. Availability and limits

The service is provided without a promise of uninterrupted availability, delivery time, data permanence, emergency response, 24/7 human moderation, automated scanning, independent age verification, or legal certification. Reports can be submitted at any time, but the currently proposed primary human coverage is 19:00–22:00 Asia/Bangkok. Final warranty, liability, indemnity, dispute, governing-law, and statutory consumer-rights terms require qualified Thai legal review.

## 6. Termination and deletion

Logging out does not delete an account. Account deletion requires recent reauthentication and confirmation, revokes ChatGun sessions and provider links, removes applicable live account/social data, and queues owned media for deletion. Security, safety, legal, D1 Time Travel, and disaster-recovery copies may remain for justified periods described in the Privacy and Deletion policies. Users should separately revoke ChatGun access in their OAuth provider settings.
