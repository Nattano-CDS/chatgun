# ChatGun Community Guidelines — public-launch-2026-10-30-rc1-draft

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED OR PUBLISHED.**

## Expected conduct

Be genuine, obtain consent, accept rejection, communicate respectfully, protect other people's privacy, and use block/report tools. A friend acceptance is permission to contact—not permission to pressure, track, copy private content, or continue after consent is withdrawn.

## Prohibited conduct

- Harassment, threats, coercion, hate, targeted humiliation, stalking, triangulation or publication of location.
- Impersonation, fraud, spam, malicious links/files, coordinated evasion, or unauthorized access.
- Sexual exploitation, grooming, child-safety abuse, NCII, sextortion, or attempts to involve anyone under 18.
- Re-sharing private messages/media without permission, evading blocks/removals, or creating replacement accounts to resume contact.
- Knowingly false or abusive reports, interference with moderation, or attempts to identify a reporter.

## Enforcement

ChatGun may warn, rate-limit, restrict contact/discovery, quarantine content, suspend or delete accounts, and escalate credible high-risk matters through the approved safety/legal process. Measures should be proportionate and recorded. Reporter identity remains confidential. An appeal must be reviewed by someone other than the original decision-maker where practicable; if no independent reviewer is available, the case remains unresolved rather than being automatically affirmed or reversed.

ChatGun is not an emergency service. Someone in immediate danger should contact appropriate local emergency services. ChatGun must not claim 24/7 review or guaranteed response while those capabilities are not genuinely staffed.
