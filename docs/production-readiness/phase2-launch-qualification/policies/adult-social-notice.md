# ChatGun Adult Social Notice — public-launch-2026-10-30-rc1-draft

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED OR PUBLISHED. Owner approval and qualified Thai legal review are required.**

ChatGun is proposed for adults aged 18 or older. The service asks for an age declaration after OAuth sign-in, calculates an adult age band, and does not retain the entered birth date. This is not independent identity or age verification. A missing, revoked, under-18, inactive, suspended, or restricted assurance prevents Social access.

## Social features and consent

Discovery is voluntary. When enabled in the foreground, ChatGun temporarily processes device coordinates and accuracy to calculate nearby eligibility. Members see only coarse distance bands; they do not receive another member's coordinates, accuracy, or location timestamp. Location and timing can still permit inference about a home, workplace, routine, or current area. Turn Discovery off or revoke browser permission to stop publication and clear the active location record.

Friend requests require an affirmative request and acceptance before text messaging. Either member may reject or cancel a pending request, remove a friend, disable incoming contact, block the other account, or report conduct. Removal or blocking immediately stops new contact and hides the conversation from the inbox; unblocking does not silently recreate friendship. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted and may be processed for delivery, abuse response, security, deletion, and legally required handling.

Online Presence is optional and off until enabled. It is a short-lived heartbeat, may be inaccurate, and does not prove that a person is viewing or responding. Ratings require server-confirmed proximity and are private to the rated member.

## Safety limits

Do not harass, threaten, stalk, infer or publish location, impersonate, exploit minors, evade blocks, share non-consensual intimate material, spam, defraud, or attempt unauthorized access. Block and report tools remain available, but ChatGun is not an emergency service and does not promise continuous human monitoring. Proposed staffed moderation remains 19:00–22:00 Asia/Bangkok until a different operating model is genuinely staffed and approved.

Media, Ratings, Sensitive Content, OTP, Demo, and any feature whose launch gate is not approved remain unavailable. Public launch must not represent automated scanning, independent age verification, legal certification, or 24/7 moderation where none exists.
