# Thai legal applicability assessment — internal, not legal approval

Assessment date: 2026-11-01. This is a technical/compliance issue-spotting exercise, not a legal opinion.

## Findings supported by authoritative sources

1. **PDPA duties apply independently of an account-review label.** The controller must maintain appropriate security, deletion/retention controls and records of processing/refusals. Data subjects have statutory rights, including complaint rights to the expert committee. An internal reconsideration cannot be described as replacing those rights. Sources: [Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562, sections 33–39 and 72–74](https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/17082307.pdf).
2. **The Digital Platform Services (DPS) Decree may be relevant.** Its definition covers an electronic intermediary that manages data to connect users for electronic transactions, whether paid or free. Whether ChatGun's social interactions fall within that definition, and which notification/exemption category applies, requires a facts-and-scale assessment before public launch. Source: [Royal Decree on Digital Platform Services B.E. 2565, sections 3 and 8](https://www.etda.or.th/getattachment/Regulator/DigitalPlatform/law/%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%8E_DP_2565.pdf.aspx?lang=th-TH).
3. **If the DPS duties apply, a complaint process and user-help channel are expressly required.** Section 25 identifies complaint handling and assistance/relief measures. Section 31 also creates ETDA's central complaint channel. Nothing in the reviewed text establishes a universal requirement that every internal account reconsideration be decided by a second employee. Sources: [DPS Decree, sections 25–26 and 31](https://www.etda.or.th/getattachment/Regulator/DigitalPlatform/law/%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%8E_DP_2565.pdf.aspx?lang=th-TH), [ETDA DPS FAQ](https://www.etda.or.th/th/contact/faq/Digital-Platform.aspx).
4. **Transparency is material.** Where the terms-disclosure provisions apply, the service must describe assistance, complaint handling and dispute-resolution processes. Therefore the UI and terms must state that reconsideration is by the same operator, give a realistic operating window and preserve external complaint routes. Source: [DPS Decree, sections 16–17](https://www.etda.or.th/getattachment/Regulator/DigitalPlatform/law/%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%8E_DP_2565.pdf.aspx?lang=th-TH).

## Conclusion

No reviewed authoritative source supports a blanket claim that ChatGun must employ permanent second staff solely to conduct ordinary same-operator reconsideration. Equally, the sources do **not** justify claiming that owner-only reconsideration is sufficient for every dispute. It is acceptable as a clearly labeled internal remedy only if it does not obstruct statutory rights, regulator complaint channels, court remedies or the separately promised independent-appeal route.

## Exceptions and unresolved questions

- Determine whether ChatGun is a DPS within section 3, whether section 8 notification thresholds/exemptions apply, and whether the Phase 1 service falls into any special/high-risk category.
- Confirm the legal controller/operator identity and Thai contact channel.
- Confirm lawful bases and retention periods for restriction evidence, reconsideration text and append-only audit records.
- Define how a privacy-rights dispute is routed without forcing the data subject through account reconsideration; PDPA complaint rights must remain explicit.
- Define external escalation for alleged illegality, imminent safety threats, regulator/court orders and conflicts where the owner cannot fairly or lawfully decide.
- Confirm consumer/contract-law implications of suspension and restoration language for the exact public business model.

Optional qualified Thai legal review remains prudent risk reduction, but this assessment does not characterize paid external counsel as automatically mandatory.
