# Proposed unpublished policy changes

Version status: internal working draft only. Do not publish or treat as owner/legal approval.

## Terms of Service — proposed account restriction section

ChatGun may restrict or suspend account access where reasonably necessary for safety, security, legal compliance or enforcement of the Community Guidelines. A restriction does not automatically delete the account or remove the member's privacy and deletion rights.

An eligible member may request **reconsideration by the original decision-maker** and provide an explanation or new information. The same ChatGun operator who made the first decision will conduct this reconsideration; it is not an independent appeal. The operator may uphold, modify or lift the restriction. Submission does not restore access automatically.

Where ChatGun offers an independent appeal, the original decision-maker is technically prohibited from deciding it. If no eligible independent reviewer is available, the independent appeal may remain pending. Neither internal route limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded, including complaints to competent authorities.

## Community Guidelines — proposed enforcement section

Enforcement may include warnings, feature restrictions or account suspension. The decision notice should state the rule category, a meaningful reason that does not reveal a reporter's identity or protected safety information, and the available review route. Reconsideration and independent appeal must use distinct names, screens and statuses.

## Account restrictions and reconsideration notice

- Reconsideration: second assessment by the original decision-maker; not independent.
- Independent appeal: assessment by a different eligible reviewer; the original decision-maker cannot decide it.
- One pending review route per restriction prevents conflicting decisions.
- Requests require authentication, recent login and a reason of at least 10 characters.
- Text evidence is used only to assess the restriction, secure the service and maintain the required decision record.
- Normal owner review occurs during the published operating schedule; no 24/7 human response is promised.
- Emergency services are not provided. Imminent danger should be directed to the appropriate emergency authority.

## Support and complaint handling

Publish one verified support/privacy channel, expected handling stages rather than a guaranteed outcome time, and statutory/regulator complaint routes that apply. Privacy-rights requests must be tracked separately from moderation reconsideration and must not require the account restriction to be lifted first.

## Moderation runbook policy

The operator must record the request, original restriction revision, decision, reason, timestamp and minimal audit link. They must not expose reporter identity, use reconsideration to bypass an independent appeal, or restore location/presence/private sharing automatically. Cases involving the operator's legal conflict, regulator direction or credible imminent harm must be escalated or paused under the incident procedure.
